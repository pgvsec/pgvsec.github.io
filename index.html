Research Focus

I am a PhD candidate in Chemical Engineering at Purdue University under the advisement of Prof. Doraiswami Ramkrishna. My research interfaces applied mathematics, nonlinear dynamical systems, and physics-informed machine learning.

My primary thrust involves deciphering the regulatory topologies that govern macrophage polarization during wound healing. By developing dynamics-preserving autoencoders and particle-mesh schemes for multi-dimensional Population Balance Equations (PBEs), I model how single-cell metabolic shifts translate to tissue-scale phenotypic distributions.
