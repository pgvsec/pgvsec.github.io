About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in the Davidson School of Chemical Engineering at Purdue University, advised by Dr. Doraiswami Ramkrishna. My research interfaces computational systems biology, nonlinear dynamical systems, and machine learning.

My work centers on mechanistic and population-balance modeling of macrophage polarization and wound healing dynamics. By integrating cybernetic control theory with physics-informed deep learning and subspace reduction methods, I build predictive architectures that simulate emergent heterogeneity and prioritize interventions across cellular networks.
